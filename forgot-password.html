How to Reset Your Password

  1. 1. Enter the email address you used to create your account.
  2. 2. Click "Send Reset Link".
  3. 3. Check your email inbox.
  4. 4. If you don't see the email, check your Spam or Junk folder.
  5. 5. Open the password reset email.
  6. 6. Click the reset link.
  7. 7. Create a new strong password.
  8. 8. Return to the login page and sign in with your new password.

Security Tips

  • • Never share your password with anyone.
  • • Never share your password reset link.
  • • Use a strong and unique password.
  • • Avoid using your name, phone number or date of birth as your password.
  • • Always reset your password from the official website.
